## 3. How it works — five steps

**One · Tell us where they rest**
Every cemetery has its own requirements: the type of memorial, the size, the material, and whether vases,
portraits, or emblems are permitted. Share the cemetery name, and section and lot if you have them. If you
don't have the paperwork, we can contact the cemetery for you.

**Two · Choose the memorial**
There are four types: flat granite, flat bronze, slant, and upright. We'll show you which ones your cemetery
allows and the sizes that fit. Most memorials honor one person. If a spouse or partner will one day rest
beside them, you can choose a companion memorial instead, with their side left open for later.

**Three · Choose the stone and design**
We have eighteen granite colors, and you can see and touch real samples at our shop. Then we choose what goes on
the stone together: a name, two dates, and one image that says who they were. Our album holds 286 designs, and if
what you picture isn't there, we'll draw it. Ceramic portraits and bronze emblems are available where your
cemetery permits them.

**Four · Receive your free written quote**
Every item is clearly listed in writing before you move forward, so you know what to expect. The quote is free,
with no obligation.

**Five · Approve your proof**
We draw the full layout to scale and send it to you. Review every name, date, and detail, ideally against a
document. Engraving begins after you approve and sign the proof.

---

## 4. After you approve

**From proof to placement**

We send the approved layout to your cemetery for its records and required review. When the memorial is
finished, it is installed according to your cemetery's requirements. Most cemeteries set the memorial
themselves. At Conejo Mountain Memorial Park, we install it ourselves. Either way, we'll tell you what to
expect at your cemetery.

**How long does it take?**
It depends on the cemetery's review, the stone you choose, and the artwork. Once we know your cemetery and
your choices, we'll give you a clear timeline.

## 5. What to bring, if you have it

Bring whatever you have. If something is missing, we can still begin.

## 10. Questions families ask

**Do I need to decide anything before I call?**
No. A name and a cemetery are enough to begin. We'll explain the choices and what your cemetery allows.

**What does a memorial cost?**
It depends on the type, size, granite, artwork, and your cemetery's requirements. We give every family a free
written quote with every item listed, before anything is decided.

**Can I see the stone before it's made?**
Yes. You'll see real granite samples at our shop and a full proof drawn to scale. Engraving begins only after
you approve and sign the proof.

**Should we choose a single or a companion memorial?**
Most families choose a single memorial for their loved one. A companion memorial is for two people, such as a
husband and wife. One side is engraved now and the other later, at our shop or at the cemetery, depending on the
cemetery's requirements. Any size can be made either way.

**Can you add a date to a stone that's already in place?**
Yes. Send us a photo of the stone and the cemetery name, and we'll explain what's possible.
