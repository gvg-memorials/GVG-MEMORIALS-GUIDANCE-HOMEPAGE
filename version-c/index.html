Losing someone is hard enough. Every cemetery has its own requirements, and every memorial is permanent. Families shouldn't have to work it out alone.

Two sets of choices

Your cemetery decides

  • The type of memorial
  • The size
  • The material
  • Whether vases, portraits, or emblems are permitted

We find out for you, even if you don't have the paperwork.

You decide

  • The granite, from eighteen colors
  • The design, from 286, or one we draw
  • The words, names, and dates
  • Single, or companion for two

Nothing is engraved until you approve and sign the proof.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to decide anything before I call?

No. A name and a cemetery are enough to begin. We'll explain the choices and what your cemetery allows.

What does a memorial cost?

It depends on the type, size, granite, artwork, and your cemetery's requirements. Every family receives a free written quote with every item listed, before anything is decided.

Can I see the stone before it's made?

Yes. Real granite samples at our shop, and a full proof drawn to scale. Engraving begins only after you approve and sign.

Should we choose a single or a companion memorial?

Most families choose a single memorial. A companion is for two people, such as a husband and wife. One side is engraved now and the other later, at our shop or at the cemetery, depending on its requirements.

What should I bring?

Whatever you have: the cemetery name and plot, their full name and dates, any words or a photo you'd like included, and any cemetery paperwork. If something is missing, we can still begin.

How long does it take?

It depends on the cemetery's review, the stone you choose, and the artwork. Once we know your cemetery and your choices, we'll give you a clear timeline.

Can you add a date to a stone that's already in place?

Yes. Send us a photo of the stone and the cemetery name, and we'll explain what's possible.

Is the second inscription done at the cemetery?

It's done at our shop or at the cemetery, depending on that cemetery's requirements.

Do you clean and restore older stones?

Yes. Send a photo and we'll tell you what cleaning or restoration would involve.
