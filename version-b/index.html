IHow it works

  1. 01

    Tell us where they rest

    Every cemetery has its own requirements: the type of memorial, the size, the material, and whether vases, portraits, or emblems are permitted. If you don't have the paperwork, we can contact the cemetery for you.

  2. 02

    Choose the memorial

    Flat granite, flat bronze, slant, or upright, within what your cemetery allows. Most memorials honor one person. If a spouse or partner will one day rest beside them, a companion memorial leaves their side open for later.

  3. 03

    Choose the stone and design

    Eighteen granite colors, with real samples at our shop. An album of 286 designs, and if what you picture isn't there, we'll draw it.

  4. 04

    Receive your free written quote

    Every item is clearly listed in writing before you move forward. No obligation.

  5. 05

    Approve your proof

    We draw the full layout to scale. Review every name, date, and detail. Engraving begins after you approve and sign.

  6. 06

    Placement

    We send the approved layout to your cemetery. Most cemeteries set the memorial themselves. At Conejo Mountain Memorial Park, we install it ourselves. Either way, we'll tell you what to expect.

VIQuestions

Do I need to decide anything before I call?

No. A name and a cemetery are enough to begin.

What does a memorial cost?

It depends on the type, size, granite, artwork, and your cemetery's requirements. Every family receives a free written quote with every item listed.

Single or companion memorial?

Most families choose a single memorial. A companion is for two people, such as a husband and wife; the second inscription is added later, at our shop or at the cemetery, depending on its requirements.

Can you add a date to an existing stone?

Yes. Send a photo of the stone and the cemetery name, and we'll explain what's possible.

What should I bring?

Whatever you have: the cemetery name and plot, their full name and dates, any words or a photo you'd like included, and any cemetery paperwork. If something is missing, we can still begin.
