IHow it works

Five steps, one at a time

1

Step one

Tell us where they rest

Every choice that follows depends on the cemetery.

Every cemetery has its own requirements: the type of memorial, the size, the material, and whether vases, portraits, or emblems are permitted. Share the cemetery name, and section and lot if you have them. If you don't have the paperwork, we can contact the cemetery for you.

Ask about your cemetery
2

Step two

Choose the memorial

Within what the cemetery allows, the choice is yours.

There are four types. We'll show you which ones your cemetery allows and the sizes that fit.

Flat granite
Sits level with the lawn.
Flat bronze
A cast bronze plate on a granite base.
Slant
Stands a few inches tall, angled to be read standing.
Upright
A headstone on its own base, where the cemetery permits one.

Most memorials honor one person. If a spouse or partner will one day rest beside them, you can choose a companion memorial instead, with their side left open for later.

3

We have eighteen granite colors, and you can see and touch real samples at our shop. Our album holds 286 designs, and if what you picture isn't there, we'll draw it. Ceramic portraits and bronze emblems are available where your cemetery permits them.

IIIAfter you approve

From proof to placement

We send the approved layout to your cemetery for its records and required review. When the memorial is finished, it is installed according to your cemetery's requirements. Most cemeteries set the memorial themselves. At Conejo Mountain Memorial Park, we install it ourselves. Either way, we'll tell you what to expect at your cemetery.

How long does it take?

It depends on the cemetery's review, the stone you choose, and the artwork. Once we know your cemetery and your choices, we'll give you a clear timeline.

IVBefore you visit

What to bring, if you have it

Bring whatever you have. If something is missing, we can still begin.

  1. The cemetery name, and the section, lot, and space number
  2. Their full name, as you'd like it engraved
  3. Dates of birth and passing
  4. Any words, a verse, or a nickname you'd like included
  5. A photo of them, for a portrait or an image based on something they loved
  6. Any paperwork from the cemetery

VIIIQuestions

Questions families ask

Do I need to decide anything before I call?

No. A name and a cemetery are enough to begin. We'll explain the choices and what your cemetery allows.

What does a memorial cost?

It depends on the type, size, granite, artwork, and your cemetery's requirements. We give every family a free written quote with every item listed, before anything is decided.

Can I see the stone before it's made?

Yes. You'll see real granite samples at our shop and a full proof drawn to scale. Engraving begins only after you approve and sign the proof.

Should we choose a single or a companion memorial?

Most families choose a single memorial for their loved one. A companion memorial is for two people, such as a husband and wife. One side is engraved now and the other later, at our shop or at the cemetery, depending on the cemetery's requirements. Any size can be made either way.

Can you add a date to a stone that's already in place?

Yes. Send us a photo of the stone and the cemetery name, and we'll explain what's possible.
