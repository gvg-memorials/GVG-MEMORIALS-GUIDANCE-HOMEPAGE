Common Questions

Before You Choose

These are the questions families usually ask before the first call.

Do I need to know the memorial style before reaching out?

No. The first conversation can narrow the choices and show what fits your cemetery, budget, and family wishes.

What memorial options can we review together?

Headstones, flat markers, upright monuments, bronze plaques, benches, custom designs, lettering, artwork, and layout proof review.

What should I bring to the first conversation?

Bring what you have: the cemetery name, paperwork, names and dates, spelling details, photos, notes, or questions. Missing details are normal, and we can still begin.

How long does a headstone or marker take?

Timing depends on cemetery approval, stone choice, artwork, and production schedule. After the first conversation, we explain the next step and what can affect timing.
